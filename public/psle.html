Before choosing

  • Appeals are a long shot for the red schools. Schools generally only consider appeals from students who meet their cut-off and only when they still have vacancies. AL 16 against a COP of 14 or 15 probably will not get in.
  • COPs move by a few points every year. The amber schools (COP 16 and 17) could land either side of 16 in the 2026 posting.
  • Use all 6 choices and mix them. MOE suggests at least 2 to 3 schools with a COP less strict than her score. Choice order affects posting.
  • Affiliated-only schools have been removed because she is not from an affiliated primary school. Every range shown is the open-intake range, which is the stricter one at schools that also have affiliated places.
  • Schools excluded: five schools whose ranges start above 16 (Canberra, Juying, Regent, Sembawang, Woodlands Ring), and schools whose last posted score was better than 14.
  • Boys' schools have been removed. That judgement comes from general knowledge, not the MOE data.
  • Verify on MOE SchoolFinder before submitting. Ranges come from a third-party table built from SchoolFinder, and one other site lists Admiralty as 15 to 20. Towns come from the MOE school directory and are not travel times.
